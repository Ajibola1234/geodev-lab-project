# My project brief

## The question

Which settlements in Akinyele Local Government Area are most vulnerable to urban flooding due to low-lying terrain, intense rainfall, poor drainage and indiscriminate solid-waste disposal?

## The data I need

- LGA boundary – OCHA Nigeria Administrative Boundaries – https://data.humdata.org/dataset/nigeria-administrative-boundaries
- Elevation – Copernicus DEM GLO-30 – https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
- Satellite imagery – Sentinel-2 Level-2A – https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/data-collections/copernicus-sentinel-missions/sentinel-2
- Rainfall – CHIRPS v3 – https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS/v3.0/
- Rivers and drainage – HydroSHEDS / HydroRIVERS – https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrorivers
- Roads and buildings – OpenStreetMap – https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html
- Population – WorldPop Nigeria – https://data.worldpop.org/repo/wopr/NGA/population/v3.0/
- Historical surface water – JRC Global Surface Water – https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/jrc-gswe-global-surface-water-explorer-v1
- Waste disposal sites – Field GPS survey and existing waste-site inventory from previous study
- Historical flood extent – Copernicus Emergency Management Service – https://emergency.copernicus.eu/data/
